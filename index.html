About BusuJJu Hardware

Busia's trusted partner in building

BusuJu Hardware began with a simple goal: to give the people of Busia a dependable place to buy quality building and construction materials. Today we are the biggest hardware supplier in Busia, stocking steel, metal, plastics, paint and much more.

Whether you are building your first home, running a large construction site or fixing something small, our team helps you choose the right materials for the job and the budget.
